Academic Programs

🎓 PUC

Our Pre-University Courses (PUC) provide a strong foundation in Arts and Commerce, preparing students for higher education and professional success. Experienced faculty and a comprehensive curriculum foster academic and personal growth.

Arts

A blend of humanities and social sciences, ideal for students interested in history, society, governance, and economics.

  • History
  • Economics
  • Sociology
  • Political Science

Commerce

For students keen on business, finance, and technology, with options in History or Computer Science.

  • History / Computer Science

🎯 Degree

Our undergraduate programs offer BA and BCom degrees, focusing on holistic education, critical thinking, and employability. Courses are designed to meet academic and industry standards.

BA

Bachelor of Arts with a multi-disciplinary approach, developing analytical and communication skills for diverse careers.

  • History
  • Economics
  • Sociology
  • Political Science
  • Optional English

BCom

Bachelor of Commerce with a focus on accounting, business, and commerce fundamentals for future professionals.
