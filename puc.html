PUC Course Details

National College Sindhanur offers Pre-University Courses (PUC) in Arts and Commerce streams. These courses are designed to provide a strong academic foundation and prepare students for higher education and professional careers. Our experienced faculty and comprehensive curriculum foster holistic growth and academic excellence.

  • Arts Stream: History, Economics, Sociology, Political Science, Optional Kannada/English
  • Commerce Stream: Business Studies, Accountancy, Economics, Computer Science, Statistics
